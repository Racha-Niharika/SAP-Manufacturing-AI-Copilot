MANUFACTURING STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
Document ID: SOP-MN-003
Title: Shop Floor Machine Maintenance & Breakdown Response

1. Purpose
This procedure defines standard maintenance protocols and emergency breakdown response actions for manufacturing equipment and CNC machines.

2. Planned Preventive Maintenance (PM)
- Preventive maintenance is executed according to weekly and monthly maintenance schedules.
- Lubrication, filter cleaning, and calibration must be logged in SAP Plant Maintenance (PM) module.
- Operating hours and sensor telemetry must be reviewed prior to sign-off.

3. Emergency Breakdown Response
When an unscheduled machine breakdown occurs:
- The operator must immediately stop the machine and press the Emergency Stop if safety is compromised.
- Tag the machine with 'Out of Service' lockout/tagout (LOTO) signage.
- Create an emergency maintenance notification in SAP PM.
- Notify the Shift Maintenance Engineer and Production Supervisor within 15 minutes.

4. Production Rerouting
If estimated repair time exceeds 2 hours:
- The Production Planner must re-route active production orders to backup or alternative work centers.
- Tooling and work-in-progress (WIP) materials must be securely transferred and recorded.

5. Return to Service
Before restarting production after maintenance or repair:
- Maintenance Engineer must complete safety interlock checks and test run.
- Quality inspector must inspect the first completed piece (First Article Inspection).
- Maintenance order must be technically completed (TECO) in SAP PM.
