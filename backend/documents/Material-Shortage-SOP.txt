MANUFACTURING STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
Document ID: SOP-MM-001
Title: Material Shortage Management

1. Purpose
This procedure explains how to handle raw material shortages during manufacturing.

2. Material Availability Verification
When a production order cannot proceed because of insufficient raw material:
- Check current unrestricted stock.
- Verify the storage location.
- Check stock in other storage locations.
- Check whether material is reserved for another order.

3. Shortage Identification
Calculate material shortage as:
Material Shortage = Required Quantity - Available Quantity

4. Corrective Actions
If sufficient stock is unavailable:
- Inform the production planning team immediately.
- Check open purchase requisitions for the material.
- Check existing purchase orders in SAP ERP.
- Verify expected supplier delivery dates.
- Contact the procurement and supply chain department to expedite orders.

5. Production Planning & Rescheduling
Production planning should evaluate whether the production schedule needs to be revised or rescheduled to prevent shop floor downtime. Alternate materials must be authorized by Quality Assurance.

6. Approval Process
Any procurement action or emergency purchase requisition must follow the company's purchase approval process. Do not create a purchase order without authorization.
