MANUFACTURING STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
Document ID: SOP-PD-002
Title: Production Order Delay Escalation & Resolution

1. Purpose
This standard operating procedure establishes guidelines for identifying, logging, escalating, and resolving production order delays on the shop floor.

2. Delay Identification & Thresholds
A production order is classified as delayed when:
- The actual start date exceeds the scheduled start date by more than 4 hours.
- Operation execution is halted for material shortage, tooling unavailability, or quality hold.
- The order planned completion date cannot be met based on current yield rates.

3. Escalation Workflow
- Level 1 (0-4 hours delay): The shop floor supervisor investigates the root cause and logs an incident note.
- Level 2 (4-12 hours delay): Escalate to the Plant Production Manager. Review resource capacity and reassign labor or machines.
- Level 3 (> 12 hours delay): Escalate to Supply Chain Operations and notify customer service if customer delivery commitments are impacted.

4. Corrective & Preventive Measures
- Prioritize delayed orders with higher customer criticality (Order Importance level).
- Perform split routing or parallel operations where machine capacity permits.
- Document reasons for delay in SAP order confirmation (scrap, setup delay, machine malfunction).

5. Documentation & Review
Weekly delay analysis meetings are conducted to review bottleneck operations, supplier delivery lead times, and continuous improvement opportunities.
